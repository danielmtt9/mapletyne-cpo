Enterprise Terms of Service & Microgrid Interconnect Agreement

Governing telemetry ingestion, high-speed automated power dispatch, wholesale market settlements, and physical substation interconnection for Mapletyne Energy Management System (EMS).

1

Master Service & Microgrid Operations Agreement

This Master Agreement authorizes Mapletyne EMS to orchestrate dynamic power dispatch across connected charging dispensers and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) according to ISO/RTO wholesale market signals. By connecting physical charging dispensers (under OCPP 2.0.1) and substation interconnection hardware, the Operator grants Mapletyne EMS direct closed-loop control to throttle, ramp, or curtail individual charging bays in accordance with local grid interconnection limits and wholesale energy arbitrage commands.

The Operator agrees to maintain continuous synchrophasor UDP multicast telemetry stream connectivity between the site substation interconnect breaker and the Mapletyne CSMS cloud cluster. Failure to broadcast telemetry for more than 3 consecutive heartbeat cycles triggers automatic local failsafe capping at 100 kW.

2

Grid Interconnection & Curtailment Protocols

Interconnection with the regional transmission organization (RTO) distribution feeder requires autonomous response to rapid frequency dips and emergency demand-response signals. All hardware profiles—including ABB Terra 184 and Alpitronic Hypercharger HYC300 units—must comply with IEEE 1547-2018 anti-islanding mandates.

warning Mandatory Utility Demand-Response Notice

Mandatory 5-minute utility demand-response curtailment protocols apply. Station dispensers may throttle to 50 kW per port during peak events without prior operator confirmation.

In the event of utility transmission grid instability exceeding 60.50 Hz or collapsing below 59.30 Hz, automated trip signals decouple local DER arrays within 16 milliseconds in adherence to Rule 21 microgrid protection standards.

3

Data Governance, Security & NERC CIP Audit Trail

All communications between on-site charging controllers, power conversion systems, and the Mapletyne EMS Cloud utilize mutual Transport Layer Security (mTLS 1.3) with client certificates rooted in a FIPS 140-2 Level 3 Hardware Security Module (HSM).

Every telemetry sample, high-frequency synchrophasor measurement, charging transaction event, and remote firmware modification is immutably signed using ECDSA (secp384r1) + SHA-384 cryptography. Operators have access to tamper-evident audit ledgers meeting NERC CIP-005, CIP-007, and ISO 27001 data integrity frameworks.
